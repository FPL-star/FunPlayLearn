Why Be a FunMaker?


When you were a child, did you ever have a question — a wild, curious, beautiful question — but kept it to yourself out of fear? Fear of sounding silly, of being wrong, of not being taken seriously? So many children carry those same questions today — but unlike you, they may never find someone who encourages them to ask out loud.


As a FunMaker, you get the chance to be the person you once needed. You’ll create spaces where children feel safe to wonder, explore, and express. You won’t be giving lectures or correcting answers — you’ll be listening, playing, sparking joy. You’ll show children that their questions matter, that their voice is heard, and that learning can be fun, messy, and full of surprises.


It’s not about teaching; it’s about protecting the spark. Every session you run, every game you co-create, every moment you spend with a child is a quiet act of belief — belief in who they are and who they might become. It’s a small gift of curiosity to someone who might be just like the younger you: wondering, waiting, hoping for someone who understands.


Be that someone — and help a child fall in love with learning.

What You’ll Gain

  • Confidence in speaking and leading group activities
  • Real-world skills in clear, compassionate communication
  • Practical experience in designing and running engaging sessions
  • A supportive network of like-minded, driven young people
  • A strong story for college, job, and fellowship applications
  • A meaningful way to enrich your gap year or complement your social work studies
  • And for many of our alumni, a valuable addition to their U.S. visa interview narratives
While most of our FunMakers are volunteers, we do offer paid fellowships based on availability, location, and program needs. These opportunities vary across time and regions — but if you're passionate, proactive, and ready to commit, we’d love to hear from you.
