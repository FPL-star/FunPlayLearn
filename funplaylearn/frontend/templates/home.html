OUR VISION

To see every Nepali child grow up in a learning environment where play sparks joy, curiosity, and wonder—shaping a generation of bold questioners and fearless explorers.

OUR MISSION

To ignite a love for learning in every child by making play the foundation of education. Rather than teaching, we aim to inspire—creating joyful, low-cost, and inclusive experiences that awaken curiosity, confidence, and collaboration. Through our network of trained young volunteers and strong partnerships with schools, we deliver structured play sessions that empower children to explore, question, and grow on their own terms. Over time, we work to shift the responsibility of nurturing playful learning to the communities themselves—by collaborating with and equipping teachers, parents, and local leaders to carry forward and sustain this approach.

OUR APPROACH

At FunPlayLearn, we believe that play is one of the most effective ways for children to learn. We recognize that traditional teaching methods often miss the fun and engaging aspect of learning. That's why we introduce exciting games into classrooms that not only entertain but also help children develop essential skills like math, problem-solving, and spatial awareness. We primarily focus on children in grades 1-4, offering a new game each week, with our sessions running from 12 to 48 weeks.

Our goal is to integrate this playful learning approach into schools. By working closely with teachers and schools, we aim to gradually shift the ownership of these activities to the schools themselves, ensuring that playful learning becomes a permanent and valued part of their teaching strategies.
